## Summary

PLAN-V1 gets the big call right: AI engineering is not the critical path, and the go-live P50 of **Sun 11 Apr 2027 does follow from the H-table** (H26 parallel run reconciled Thu 8 Apr → cutover → Sun 11 Apr). Every weekday printed in §1 and §10 is correct (35 dates checked). Ramadan 1448 is plausible: Umm al-Qura gives 1 Ramadan = 8 Feb 2027, 29 Ramadan = 8 Mar, 1 Shawwal = 9 Mar, and 10 Dhu al-Hijjah = 16 May. Libyan moon sighting can shift these by ±1 day.

The plan cannot be executed as written, for four reasons:

1. **The build environment does not match the operating model.** This session has **no Docker daemon** (`docker ps` → "no such file … docker.sock"), and it runs on **4 vCPU and 15 GB RAM**, shared by every subagent. G6, G19, the testcontainers harness, F01's Docker Compose and the "local Docker Postgres per builder worktree" all assume Docker. The plan also assumes `main` merges, wave/review branches, CODEOWNERS and CI on every branch. The session has one designated dev branch (`claude/gallant-archimedes-b70o62`).
2. **The wave table breaks the protocol's own rule** (AGENT-PROTOCOL §0.2: no WP may depend on another WP's unmerged output in the same wave). It does so explicitly in W4 and W5, and implicitly in about 12 more places. The auditor's check A-1.9 would reject the W4 and W5 briefs.
3. **D00 is said not to block W1–W6** (§1, §14 A-9). Yet D00's own "Blocks" column names W1, W2, W3 and W4 (D00-05/07/11/15/16/20/21/24), and H1 approval has P50 Sun 8 Nov (day 7).
4. **PLAN-V1 and AGENT-PROTOCOL disagree** on ADR numbering and paths, migration numbering, wave IDs, PG version, coverage thresholds, npm scope, WP-00 vs F01, synchronous vs async audit, and whether `SECURITY DEFINER` is allowed outside `packages/auth` and `packages/db/rls`. Builders given both documents get contradictory instructions.

Beyond these, the plan has:

- **Material gaps**:
  - M12 payables, fixed assets and budgets have no WP.
  - §13 monitoring and access-review controls have no WP.
  - Backups and the restore test are needed at G-P1 but are only built in P3.
  - §16 UAT scripts, runbooks and the cutover runbook have no WP.
  - Nothing governs real-data handling during ARIMA trial loads.
- **Unenforceable controls**:
  - Agent-written evidence logs.
  - CODEOWNERS locks with no PR flow.
  - Same-model reviewers checking AI-authored golden tests.
- **No budget** for tokens or cost, or for human operator hours.
- **i-text review misstated.** C-08's P1 estimate (3,000–5,000 keys, 200–500 reviewer-hours) is used as if it were the whole-system figure. C-08 says the full system is 10,000–15,000 keys, so i-text review is near-critical, not slack.

Two D00 defaults need correction before W7:

- **D00-01 (`wakala_basis = collected`).** This overstates the PTF surplus whenever receivables are outstanding at year end.
- **D00-09 (operator policy fees).** These have no SSB approver.

**Counts:** 4 BLOCKER · 15 HIGH · 14 MEDIUM · 6 LOW (39 findings).
